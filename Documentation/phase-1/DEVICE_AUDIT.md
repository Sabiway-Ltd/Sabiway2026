# Phase 1 device and responsiveness audit

## Web viewports

- 360 × 800 — small Android
- 390 × 844 — modern iPhone
- 768 × 1024 — tablet portrait
- 1024 × 768 — tablet landscape
- 1440 × 900 — desktop

## Native targets

- Android 7+ baseline
- iOS 16.4+ baseline
- Phone portrait is primary; phone landscape and tablet layouts are required checks.

## Evidence policy

A phase is not complete until its changed journeys are checked at all relevant viewports, keyboard/focus behavior is verified, text scaling is reviewed, and failures are attached to the phase PR.
